Yearly

Privacy Policy

Effective as of 26 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Yearly (the "Application"), a web app at yearly.adrianobp.dev that shows your Google Calendar one year per page, collects, uses, stores and shares your information. Yearly is an open source project run by an individual developer (the "Service Provider") and is provided "as is".

1. Information the Application collects and why

  • Your Google account details (name, profile picture and email address), to show who is signed in.
  • Your calendars and events, to draw them in the year view and to create, edit and delete events when you ask it to. They are read and changed straight from your browser and are not copied to the Service Provider's server.
  • Your Yearly settings (for example which calendars are shown, and any optional API keys), kept in your browser and synced through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • A sign-in token, kept encrypted on the Service Provider's server so you stay signed in, as described in section 3.
  • Notification data, only if you turn notifications on, as described in section 4.
  • Your IP address, which the Service Provider's server sees on each request and keeps in memory only to limit abuse. It is not written to disk or logged.

The Application has no analytics, tracking or advertising, does not collect your location, and does not use your information to contact you or for marketing.

2. Sharing

Your information is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone except:

  • Google, which hosts your calendar and Drive data and serves the Application (Firebase Hosting). See Google's privacy policy.
  • The optional services you choose to connect, described in section 4.
  • When required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process.

3. Google data

Google Services provide access to the following Google user data (referred as scopes):

User details

  • Publicly shared personal information
  • Primary email address

Calendar details

  • View all Google Calendars associated with the account
  • View and edit events across all Google Calendars associated with the account

Drive app data

  • A hidden folder in your Google Drive that only the Application can see, used to sync settings between your devices. The Application cannot see your other Drive files.

Your calendar is read and changed straight from your device. To keep you signed in, the Service Provider's server keeps the long-lived sign-in token Google issues, encrypted with a key that only your signed-in device holds, and uses it only to give that device a new hour-long access token. It never reads your calendar itself. Logging out revokes the token at Google and deletes it, and a token unused for 180 days is deleted automatically. You can also remove the Application's access at any time from your Google Account.

Google user data is used only to show your calendars and events in the year view, to create, edit and delete events you ask for, and to sync your Yearly settings. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described in this policy.

Yearly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not used for advertising, not sold to data brokers or anyone else, not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending, and not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models. The optional "Ask your calendar" feature sends your question and event details to Google Gemini only when you ask it to, using your own Gemini API key, solely to answer that question.

4. Optional integrations

Some features can use third-party services with an API key that you create and add in Settings. All of them are optional, and the Application works without them.

  • Keys and request counts are stored in your browser and synced through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so they work on all your devices. They are not sent to the Service Provider.
  • Requests go directly from your device to the service the key belongs to, and may include event details such as a location, a date or a flight number. Ask your calendar sends your question with the titles, dates, places and calendars of the year's events to Google Gemini.
  • A few services can't be reached from a browser (football-data.org, sometimes Ticketmaster). Those requests, including your key for that service, pass through the Service Provider's server, which forwards them without storing or logging them.
  • Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy: Google Gemini, TomTom, Ticketmaster, Aviationstack, football-data.org.
  • Notifications are off until you turn them on for a device in Settings. When they're on, each time the Application opens on that device it works out alerts for the next 3 days and sends them to the Service Provider's server: an event's title, the time to alert, and a short message (for example a leave-by time, a nearby match or a weather warning). The server also keeps that device's push address from its browser. Each alert is deleted once it's sent. Turning notifications off or logging out deletes the device and its alerts, and a device that hasn't opened the Application for 60 days is deleted automatically. Notifications are delivered through the browser maker's push service (Google, Mozilla, Microsoft or Apple), encrypted so that only your device can read them.
  • On Gemini's free tier, Google may use the requests you send to improve its products.
  • Anyone with access to your browser, or any script running in the Application, could read stored keys. Remove a key from Settings at any time.

5. Your choices

You can stop the Application from accessing your data at any time by logging out, which revokes its access at Google and deletes your sign-in token and notification data from the Service Provider's server, or by removing Yearly from your Google Account. You can delete the hidden app folder from Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".

6. Data retention policy

  • Your calendars and events stay in your Google Calendar. The Service Provider's server never stores them.
  • Your settings stay in your browser and in the hidden app folder in your Google Drive until you delete them.
  • The encrypted sign-in token is deleted when you log out, or automatically after 180 days without use.
  • Notification alerts are deleted once sent. A device's notification data is deleted when you turn notifications off or log out, or automatically after 60 days without use.
  • IP addresses are only held in memory and are never stored.

To have any data the Service Provider holds about you deleted, log out of the Application or email adriano.boccardo@gmail.com, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case the Service Provider discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider (adriano.boccardo@gmail.com) so that they will be able to take the necessary actions.

8. Security

  • All traffic between your browser, Google and the Service Provider's server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The sign-in token is encrypted with a key that only your signed-in device holds, so it can't be read from the server alone.
  • Data on the server is stored in files only the server process can read, on a machine only the Service Provider can access.
  • Notifications are end-to-end encrypted so that only your device can read them.
