How Yearly uses your Google data

You sign in with your Google account. Yearly asks for access to your Google Calendar so it can show your calendars and events in the year view and create, edit and delete the events you ask it to. It also uses a hidden app folder in your Google Drive to sync your Yearly settings between devices, and cannot see your other Drive files. Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
